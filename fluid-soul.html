Fluid Soul connects creative tools, artwork, forms, product paths, and idea systems into one experience. The goal is simple: start anywhere, move useful work between tools, and keep the process connected instead of scattering it across isolated apps.

1. ENTERStart with an idea, phrase, artwork, need, or creative problem.
2. BUILDUse the right gadget or creative system to develop it.
3. MOVECarry useful output into the next tool, product path, or decision.
4. RETURNKeep work organized so the system becomes a workspace, not a one-off novelty.
