Depth 01 · World Law

The environment may lie to physics. The portfolio never lies to the visitor.

Ocean of Ink can flow upward, split into rooms, hide doors in currents, or let a bubble fall. The work inside it remains real. Experiments are called experiments. Concepts are called concepts. Nothing is dressed up as a client, award, metric, or project that never existed.

If we can imagine a beautiful reason for it to happen, physics is negotiable.

Depth 02 · Inhabitants on Shift

Somebody still has to keep this impossible place running.

The people who belong here are recurring world inhabitants, not portfolio decoration. They have jobs, routines, bad timing, and absolutely no concern that the ocean around them is made of ink.

Recurring Sighting

The Pool Guy

Gets into the tank and cleans it. The tank is an Ocean of Ink. This does not appear to have discouraged him. Ink eventually drifts back over the clean spot.

Tank maintenance · active

Systems Inspection

The Tech Guy

Moves through the world checking lights, panels, currents, sensors, and things that should not require sensors. He records impossible readings with professional disappointment and keeps walking.

Inspection route · ongoing

World Inhabitant

The Fish Guy

Depth 03 · Code Tide

The world has wiring. You just don't have to stare at it.

Code Tide is where atmosphere and construction meet. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, interaction, and systems are part of the environment, but they support the experience instead of becoming the scenery.

Show them the lantern. Don't show them the wiring.
