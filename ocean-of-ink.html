Creative Direction

The work begins before the finished piece.

I direct the relationships between ideas, visuals, words, technology, interaction, atmosphere, and personality. The question is not only “What can I make?” It is “What should this become, what belongs, what needs to leave, and what should someone experience when it is finished?”

01 / Shape

Find the idea.

Define what the project is really trying to say, what deserves emphasis, and what someone should notice first.

02 / Edit

Know what leaves.

Add, remove, simplify, reframe, and pivot when something works technically but fails to communicate the right thing.

03 / Connect

Make the pieces belong.

Bring visual language, writing, systems, interaction, photography, artwork, and technology into one coherent experience.

04 / Reveal

Let personality through.

Curiosity, humor, word association, experimentation, and unexpected connections are not decoration. They help determine the direction.

That is the level I want people to encounter first: the thinking, decisions, pivots, and systems behind the work. The portfolio is evidence of that direction, not the entire definition of it.
