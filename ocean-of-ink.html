Depth 01 · World

The map itself becomes the artwork.

Ocean of Ink is not a five-item menu pretending to be a world. It is the connective environment around the work, the systems, the rooms, the language, and the experiments.

Depth 02 · Creative Direction

The work begins before the finished piece.

The direction lives in the choices: what the project should become, what belongs, what leaves, how words and visuals connect, and what someone should experience when they move through it.

01 / Shape

Find the idea.

Define what the project is really trying to say and what deserves emphasis.

02 / Edit

Know what leaves.

Remove, simplify, reframe, and pivot when something works technically but not creatively.

03 / Connect

Make it belong.

Bring visual language, writing, interaction, photography, artwork, and technology together.

04 / Reveal

Let personality through.

Curiosity, humor, word association, experimentation, and unexpected connections stay visible.
