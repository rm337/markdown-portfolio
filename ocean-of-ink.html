Creative Direction

The work begins before the finished piece.

I direct the relationships between ideas, visuals, words, technology, interaction, atmosphere, and personality. The question is not only what can be made, but what the experience should become.

01 / Shape

Find the idea.

Define what deserves emphasis and what someone should notice first.

02 / Edit

Know what leaves.

Add, remove, simplify, reframe, and pivot when the direction needs to change.

03 / Connect

Make the pieces belong.

Bring visual language, writing, systems, interaction, photography, artwork, and technology together.

04 / Reveal

Let personality through.

Curiosity, humor, experimentation, and unexpected connections help determine the direction.

The portfolio is evidence of the direction, not the whole definition of it.
