# Fluid Soul Gadget Product Rules

Every existing and future Fluid Soul gadget must earn its place through clear differentiation and honest user value.

## The four gates

### 1. Clear user
State who the gadget is for and what job, problem, or creative task it helps them accomplish.

### 2. Clear difference
State why someone would choose this gadget instead of a generic alternative. Differentiation can come from speed, access, privacy, price, workflow, specialization, creative method, interoperability, or another real advantage. More features alone do not count.

### 3. Clear trust
The promise must match the working product. Do not imply public access, automation, saved data, commerce, integrations, or capabilities that have not been verified.

### 4. Clear launch reason
A gadget moves to the launch set only when its value and differentiation are easy to understand. Otherwise it remains on the roadmap and continues developing.

## Required product statement

Every gadget should be able to complete this sentence in plain English:

> Unlike other tools, this one ______.

If that sentence is vague, generic, or only says the tool has more features, the differentiation needs more work.

## Fluid Soul umbrella tenet

**Technology should make creative people more themselves.**

This tenet should guide product behavior, positioning, launch decisions, privacy choices, and the relationship between AI and the user.

## Launch discipline

Do not launch every experiment at once. Start with tools that are easiest to understand, trust, and sell. Hold other tools for the roadmap and introduce them over time through useful releases, organic marketing, and word of mouth.

## Current launch set

- T-Shirt Design Lab: develops the language and concept before the mockup stage.
- Network Radar: lightweight relationship prioritization built around attention, timing, local data, and manual judgment rather than heavy CRM administration.
- Double-Taker: deliberately explores perceptual and linguistic friction that makes a phrase earn a second read.

## Roadmap differentiation targets

- Creative Intelligence Vault: preserve idea lineage and relationships instead of becoming a flat notes bucket.
- Studio Artifact Foundry: translate one creative seed across multiple physical product directions without losing the original concept.
- Grand Unified Studio: move useful work between specialist tools instead of trapping output inside isolated apps.
- Robert's Puns / Case Files: turn a traceable language corpus into reusable source material for products, naming, writing, and campaigns.

## Standing rule

These rules apply to all current gadgets and every gadget added later. A gadget is not front-line ready until its user, difference, trust promise, and launch reason are clear.
