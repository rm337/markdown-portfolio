## Product Family Rule

**Coasters and wood pieces are separate product families.**

- Coasters belong only in the **Coasters** section.
- Wood panels, wood rounds, and other wood art belong only in the **Wood Pieces** section.
- Neither category may be used as a substitute for the other.
- The generic repository label `Inkspirations Coasters / Functional Art` is not an individual product and is not counted as inventory.
