Focused launch set

Useful first.
Different on purpose.

Every Fluid Soul gadget now has to earn its place. It must solve a clear problem for a clear person and have a believable reason to choose it instead of a generic alternative. More features alone do not count as differentiation.

Technology should make creative people more themselves.
1. CLEAR USERWe can name who it helps and what job they are trying to get done.
2. CLEAR DIFFERENCEIts advantage must be understandable through speed, access, privacy, price, workflow, specialization, or another real distinction.
3. CLEAR TRUSTThe promise must match what the tool actually does. No inflated claims, fake access, or hidden complexity.
4. CLEAR LAUNCH REASONIt launches because it deserves attention now. Otherwise it stays on the roadmap until it does.
