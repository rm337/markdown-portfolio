Almost Four-Dimensional

Water that seems to move around you.

The effect uses layered currents, moving caustic light, refraction-like distortion, drifting transparency, depth, and slow bubbles to create the illusion that water is passing through the screen rather than simply sitting behind it. Motion is deliberately restrained so the room remains peaceful.
