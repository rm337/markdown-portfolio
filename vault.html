What it does

The Vault keeps ideas from disappearing into notes, chats, screenshots and half-finished thoughts. It can surface forgotten ideas, collide two unrelated thoughts, expand one idea across different formats, and trace the lineage between the original spark and what it becomes.

Random ResurrectionPulls an older forgotten idea back into play.
Idea Connection MapShows how one thought turns into another.
CollideSmashes two ideas together to find new directions.
ExpandTurns one seed into content, merch, gadgets, visuals and more.

The system keeps the original idea and Robert’s direction separate from AI contribution, so the creative source stays visible instead of getting blended into one anonymous output.
